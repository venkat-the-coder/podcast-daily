## 🎯 Quick Tips for Maximum Engagement

### **Posting Best Practices**

1. **Optimal Posting Times**:
   - Tuesday-Thursday: 8-10 AM, 12-2 PM
   - Avoid: Weekends, early mornings, late nights

2. **Use Line Breaks**:
   - Short paragraphs (1-2 sentences)
   - Bullet points with symbols (→ ✅ ❌)
   - Emojis for visual breaks

3. **Hook Formula**:
   - First line = Make them stop scrolling
   - Use numbers, questions, or bold statements

4. **End with CTA**:
   - Ask a question
   - Request engagement
   - Include link (but not in first comment)

5. **Hashtags**:
   - 3-5 relevant hashtags
   - Mix popular + niche
   - Put at end of post

### **Engagement Strategy**

- Reply to EVERY comment within first hour
- Ask follow-up questions
- Thank people for sharing
- Be authentic, not salesy
